# PM Corrective Rules — Cycle 008

## Trigger

The operator identified that Jira updates were repeatedly focused on the same governance Scrum items while product stories touched by code changes were not consistently updated.

## Finding

The concern was valid. Governance blockers were tracked, but Product/Epic story mapping was incomplete in recent cycles.

## Permanent correction

1. Every cycle must perform a changed-files-to-Jira-story audit before final reply.
2. Every touched product story must be commented or explicitly excluded with reason.
3. Governance-only Jira updates are forbidden when product code changed.
4. Full story tickets remain In Progress unless the complete source DOD is met.
5. PM Pack zip and Cycle response artifacts remain mandatory every final cycle handoff.

## Verification

Cycle 008 created SCRUM-250 and updated missed Cycle 007 product tickets including Collection, Analysis, Dashboard, and Reporting stories.
