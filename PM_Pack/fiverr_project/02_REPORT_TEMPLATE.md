# Fiverr Report Template

## Naming Convention
`FIVERR_REPORT_<NICHE_SLUG>_<YYYYMMDD>.md`

## Section 1 ? Executive Summary
- Niche recommendation decision (GO / CONDITIONAL_GO / HOLD)
- Confidence level and key risks
- 30-day opportunity estimate

## Section 2 ? Demand Signals
- Search interest trend overview
- Buyer request frequency and quality
- Seasonal and event-driven demand notes

## Section 3 ? Competition Landscape
- Top seller concentration
- Offer and pricing clusters
- White-space opportunities

## Section 4 ? Unit Economics
- Expected package pricing by tier
- Conversion assumptions and scenario ranges
- Revenue potential and payback expectations

## Section 5 ? Go-To-Market Plan
- Offer architecture
- Positioning statement
- 14-day launch sequence

## Section 6 ? Decision and Next Actions
- Final score and recommendation
- Validation tasks for next cycle
- Required artifacts and owners
