# Fiverr Niche Queue GTM

## Queue Strategy
Queue starts with 20 niches split across:
- Tier A (priority 1-10): immediate monetization and demand-proven categories
- Tier B (priority 11-20): adjacent expansion opportunities

## Go-To-Market Phases
1. Validate demand + buyer urgency
2. Score competition and entry feasibility
3. Package and position offer variants
4. Launch micro-offers and collect conversion evidence
5. Scale winners into recurring monthly offers

## Revenue Projection Model
- Conservative: 3 retained clients/month at $300 average package
- Base: 6 retained clients/month at $450 average package
- Aggressive: 10 retained clients/month at $650 average package
