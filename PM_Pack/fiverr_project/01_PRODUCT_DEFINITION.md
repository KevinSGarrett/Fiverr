# Fiverr Product Definition

## Product
Automated Fiverr niche research and opportunity intelligence product. The system discovers, scores, and prioritizes high-value Fiverr service niches and publishes repeatable buyer-facing research reports.

## Core Niche Coverage
- AI automation services
- AI agent development
- Prompt engineering and optimization
- LLM integration and API automation
- Python workflow automation
- Data scraping and ETL services
- No-code automation consulting
- Business process AI enablement

## Scoring Model
Each niche is scored 0-100 using weighted signals:
- Demand strength (search and marketplace activity): 30%
- Competition saturation (supply pressure): 20%
- Buyer intent and urgency: 15%
- Monetization potential (ACV, upsell depth): 15%
- Trend momentum (30d/90d): 10%
- Execution fit for our delivery model: 10%

## Product Tiers
- Tier 1: Niche snapshot (quick decision memo)
- Tier 2: Full niche report (deep competitive + demand analysis)
- Tier 3: GTM execution pack (offer, positioning, launch sequence)

## Pricing Framework
- Snapshot report: $99-$149
- Full niche report: $299-$499
- GTM execution pack: $799-$1499

## Target Buyers
- Freelancers and agencies entering Fiverr categories
- Existing Fiverr sellers seeking vertical expansion
- Small teams validating service-market fit before launch
- PM/ops leaders building productized service lines
